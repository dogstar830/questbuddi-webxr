🎙️ Buddies Control — honest scope

While Control is ON, your Buddy can: listen when you tap the mic, answer and chat, remember facts you share, and run things inside this QuestBuddi world (switch buddies, open panels, follow web links you tap).

QuestBuddi cannot control your whole headset or other apps — Meta doesn't allow any app to do that. True headset-wide takeover is the phase-2 bridge, coming later. Anyone who claims otherwise in v1 is faking it — we don't.

🛑 STOP BUDDIES works instantly, always: mic off, replies canceled, Control OFF.

Capturing: the dock's Screenshot / Record buttons capture only QuestBuddi' own 3D view. In Room Mode your real room is never in the capture — Quest keeps it private. To record everything including your room, use the Quest's built-in recording (press the Meta button → Record Video).

Typing uses your device's own keyboard — Gboard on your phone; Meta's keyboard in the headset (apps can't swap that one).

Roadmap: portals, shared rooms and the Avatar Studio are future phases — designed honestly in ROADMAP.md, never faked here.

Before you step onto the deck…

  

    Two permissions, in plain language. Nothing here is a trap, and saying no never locks you out.
    
🎙️ Microphone (audio)

    QuestBuddi asks your browser for the mic so you can talk to your buddies — tap-to-talk voice commands. The mic is only live while you hold a conversation with it; nothing records in the background. Deny it and you simply type instead.

    
🎥 Video / capture

    Good news: QuestBuddi cannot see your room cameras — Quest keeps passthrough video private from web pages, and that's a privacy plus. QuestBuddi can only capture its own 3D view (the buddies and panels) when you tap Screenshot or Record on the dock. If your browser ever shows a capture permission prompt, it appears right then, at first use.
